Three Revolutionary Partnership Models

Whether you want guaranteed capital return, instant upfront liquidity, or recurring 1st-of-month rent without tenant hassles.

Guaranteed Capital Return

5-to-10 Year Capital Lease

We lease your property upfront for 5 or 10 years. You receive the full capital return at the end of the lease period, while Croww furnishes, operates, and sub-leases the home.

  • ✓ Full principal returned at lease end
  • ✓ Zero repair or maintenance deductions
  • ✓ Institutional registered lease deed

Immediate Upfront Liquidity

Modified Upfront Lease

  • ✓ Zero repayment liability at end of tenure
  • ✓ Asset handed back in pristine state
